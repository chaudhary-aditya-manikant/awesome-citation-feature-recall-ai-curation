# Prompt 12 — Contribution and Novelty Boundary (updated 5 November 2026)

Defensible contributions:
1. A fail-closed, reproducible pipeline for a single-feature citation ablation with group-conditional Recall@K, grouped splitting, paired cluster bootstrap and explicit provenance stamping.
2. An audited 2,790-record OpenAlex candidate pool with documented metadata, duplicate/version and administrative decisions.
3. An exploratory, pool-relative observation that a citation feature lowered recall of rarely cited relevant records (7 records; provisional labels).
4. A quantified feasibility lesson: with ~2.5% relevance prevalence and few low-citation positives, confirmatory group-conditional recall needs far larger or differently designed pools.

Not claimed: novelty of studying citation bias or popularity bias in scholarly search (well established — Beel & Gipp 2009; Färber et al. 2023; Rovira et al. 2021); any confirmatory effect; any language effect; any claim about commercial search engines.
